\section{Further certificate consequences}
\label{app:theory-discussion}

\begin{remark}[What the certificate buys the search]
\label{rem:relaxation-window-details}
Because $1/\theta_G=2-c_G$ for every base scheme, the certified
over-relaxation window is exactly $\rho\in(0,2-c_G)$: the textbook interval
$(0,2)$ for the schemes with no forward step, shrinking as the forward step
consumes the budget. For Condat--V\~u this window is not a conservative
surrogate. \citet[Theorem~3.1]{Condat2013Primal} proves convergence of the
same relaxed iteration for
$\rho_n\in\bigl(0,\delta\bigr)$ with
$\delta=2-\frac{\beta}{2}\bigl(\frac1\tau-\sigma\|L\|^2\bigr)^{-1}$, where
$\beta=L_f$ is the Lipschitz constant of the smooth gradient. Multiplying
numerator and denominator by $\tau$ gives
$\delta=2-c_\star$, where
$c_\star:=\frac{\tau L_f}{2(1-\tau\sigma\|L\|^2)}$ is the forward-step
constant computed with the exact operator norm. Since $\ell\geq\|L\|$, it follows
that $c_\star\leq c_G$ and hence $2-c_G\leq\delta$. The certified window
therefore coincides with Condat's when $\ell=\|L\|$, and is contained in it
when $\ell>\|L\|$. The discovered policy of Section~\ref{sec:results}
illustrates this effect. The certified textbook default
$(\tau,\sigma)=(0.5,0.186)$ has $c_G\approx0.97$ and therefore admits almost
no over-relaxation at all, $\rho<1.03$, whereas the evolved
$(\tau,\sigma)=(0.05,1.0)$ has $c_G=0.042$ and admits $\rho<1.96$. Shrinking
the primal step does not merely satisfy the gate, it widens the interval the
relaxation gene may search in, and the champion sits near its new edge at
$\rho=1.9$, with $\rho\,\theta=0.97$.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Halpern genomes.}
A certificate also determines the nonexpansive map used by the Halpern
wrapper. The map $S_G=I+\theta_G^{-1}(T_G-I)$ is nonexpansive in
$\|\cdot\|_{H_G}$
with $\operatorname{Fix}S_G=\operatorname{Fix}T_G$. The wrapped iteration is
not averaged, so Corollary~\ref{cor:convergence} does not apply. Classical
Halpern theory instead gives its guarantee using the certificate for $T_G$.
Under the admitted schedules, it converges to
$P^{H_G}_{\operatorname{Fix}T_G}(z_{\rm anc})$, the $\|\cdot\|_{H_G}$-metric
projection of the anchor, and the canonical schedule anchored at $z_0$ drives
the squared residual at $O(1/k^2)$, exactly optimal in the black-box oracle
model of \citet{ParkRyu2022Exact}
(Proposition~\ref{prop:halpern}, Appendix~\ref{app:theory}). Thus, the
Halpern gene changes the selected fixed point and the residual rate, while
preserving convergence.

\paragraph{Formulations and implementations.}
Relocating constraints or applying fixed equilibration can change the
splitting and hence $T_G$, but need not change the underlying optimization
problem. For each formulation, the gate must check its own scheme inequality
with a valid bound on its transformed operator; its decoder must recover a
solution of the original problem. The solution-transfer rule is made explicit
in Proposition~\ref{prop:formulation-transfer} of Appendix~\ref{app:theory}.
In particular, an exact projection may serve as a proximal map after
relocation; replacing it by synthesized code preserves the theorem only if
that code implements the same map. Finite contract tests against a reference
projection support admission of the implementation, but do not prove that
equality for every input. Precision and fusion also change the implemented
computation, not the certificate for the ideal map. The mathematical guarantee
must therefore be distinguished from tests of numerical kernels and from the
acceptance check on each returned point.

\paragraph{Safeguarded schedules.}
Corollary~\ref{cor:convergence} assumes exact arithmetic and exact atoms.
Returned numerical points are separately checked by the acceptance criterion
of Section~\ref{sec:exp:protocol}; the TV cell also checks its runtime duality
gap (Section~\ref{sec:methods}). Free-form
schedules, including the $\mathrm{switch}$ gene of Section~\ref{sec:mixing},
run outside $\mathcal G$ under a summable-error safeguard: the
monitor accepts a step $T_Gz_k+e_k$ only if $\|e_k\|_{H_G}\leq\eta_k$ for a
budget satisfying $\sum_k\eta_k<\infty$. Otherwise, the monitor uses the
certified step. This rule preserves convergence to a fixed point
(Lemma~\ref{lem:safeguard}, Appendix~\ref{app:theory}). This extension is not
used in the experiments. All reported runs have $e_k=0$.

For a fixed admissible formulation and exact atoms, every genome admitted
from $\mathcal G$ thus converges in exact arithmetic. Fitness selects for
time to an accepted answer; it does not establish the convergence guarantee.
