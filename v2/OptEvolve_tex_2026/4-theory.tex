\section{Convergence by construction}
\label{sec:theory}

For problem~\eqref{eq:composite}, assume finite-dimensional spaces, convex $f$ with $L_f$-Lipschitz gradient, closed proper convex $g,h$, a nonempty saddle set, exact required proximal maps, and a supplied $\ell\ge\|L\|$. For a positive-definite metric $H$, an operator is $\theta$-averaged if $T=(1-\theta)I+\theta S$, with $S$ nonexpansive in $\|\cdot\|_H$ and $0<\theta<1$ \citep{RyuYin2022LargeScale}. Let $\mathcal X^\star$ be the primal solution set and $\operatorname{dist}_H(z,C)=\min_{c\in C}\|z-c\|_H$.

\begin{definition}[Construction certificate]
\label{def:certificate}
A certificate for the exact iteration $T_G$ is $(H_G,\theta_G,\pi_G)$ with $H_G\succ0$ and (C1) $T_G$ $\theta_G$-averaged in $\|\cdot\|_{H_G}$, (C2) $\operatorname{Fix}T_G\ne\emptyset$, and (C3) $\pi_G(\operatorname{Fix}T_G)\subseteq\mathcal X^\star$. The decoder $\pi_G$ maps a fixed point to a solution.
\end{definition}

The gate admits the grammar
\begin{equation}
G::=\mathrm{base}_s(p)\mid\mathrm{relax}_{\rho}(G'),\qquad
s\in\{\mathrm{fbs},\mathrm{drs},\mathrm{dys},\mathrm{pdhg},\mathrm{cv}\},
\label{eq:grammar}
\end{equation}
where $p$ denotes stepsizes and $\rho>0$ relaxes the base iteration. The fixed metric may differ between genomes but not across steps of one solve. Table~\ref{tab:certificates} specifies the sufficient gate conditions; additional formulation, numerical, and device choices are not terms of this grammar.

\begin{theorem}[Gate soundness]
\label{thm:soundness}
For an admissible formulation under the assumptions above, every $G$ generated by~\eqref{eq:grammar} and accepted by the gate carries a construction certificate for its \emph{exact} iteration. For a base scheme, Table~\ref{tab:certificates} gives $H_G$ and $\theta_G=1/(2-c_G)$; for relaxation, the gate requires $\rho\theta_{G'}<1$ and yields $(H_{G'},\rho\theta_{G'},\pi_{G'})$.
\end{theorem}
The proof is induction over the grammar, using the base-scheme and relaxation rules in Appendix~\ref{app:theory}.

\begin{corollary}[Convergence and residual rate]
\label{cor:convergence}
For certified $T_G$, the exact iterates $z_{k+1}=T_Gz_k$ converge from any initial state to a fixed point decoding to a solution. Their step residual is nonincreasing and satisfies
\begin{equation}
\|z_{k+1}-z_k\|_{H_G}^2\le
\frac{\theta_G}{(1-\theta_G)(k+1)}
\operatorname{dist}_{H_G}^2(z_0,\operatorname{Fix}T_G).
\label{eq:residual-rate}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}
This follows from averagedness and the fixed-point correspondence (Appendix~\ref{app:theory}). The rate concerns the exact step residual, not an objective-gap guarantee for floating-point code.

\begin{remark}[The search can spend certified relaxation slack]
\label{rem:relaxation-window}
The base certificate permits $0<\rho<2-c_G$. For Condat--V\~u the evolved TV parameters $(\tau,\sigma,\rho)=(0.05,1.0,1.9)$ satisfy this condition: a smaller primal step widens the admissible relaxation range. Appendix~\ref{app:theory-discussion} compares this window with the classical condition.
\end{remark}

A formulation must additionally preserve solutions and pass the gate with its transformed operator bound (Appendix~\ref{app:theory}). Finite projection and kernel tests do not establish the required exact-atom assumption on all inputs; a returned numerical point instead faces the independent acceptance check of Section~\ref{sec:exp:protocol}. Halpern and safeguarded-step guarantees, neither used in the reported results, are detailed in Appendix~\ref{app:theory-discussion}.
