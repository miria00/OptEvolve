\begin{abstract}
Canonical optimization algorithms each excel in distinct regimes, yet
identifying the optimal combination of formulation, algorithm, and hardware
strategy for a target instance remains non-obvious. While recent algorithmic
discovery frameworks utilize LLM-guided evolutionary search, naive source-code
mutation fails to provide structural guarantees of mathematical convergence. We
introduce \textbf{OptEvolve}, an automated framework for optimization modeling
and verified solver synthesis. OptEvolve synthesizes and selects high-performing solvers for a target problem
family by co-optimizing mathematical formulations, algorithmic schemes, and
hardware-level operator implementations. Convergence is a design
constraint in OptEvolve's monotone-operator calculus: a symbolic gate checks
sufficient conditions for typed base-scheme and relaxation genomes before
evaluation; synthesized projections pass separate contract tests. An empirical
cost model guides the search
across hyperparameters and stopping criteria, while optimizing GPU iteration
fusion tiles on target devices. OptEvolve matches or beats domain-specialist
solvers on structured problems and outperforms general-purpose baselines where
no specialist exists. Furthermore, its targeted CUDA fusion tiles outperform XLA
by up to $2.7\times$, and the framework generalizes effectively across unseen,
held-out QP, LP, and SOCP benchmarks.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
We will release a pip-installable implementation, reproduction scripts, solver
genomes and certificates, configurations, seeds, and data manifests.
Public data sources and evaluation conventions are documented in the companion
experiment log (Sections~\ref{LOG-app:log:data} and~\ref{LOG-app:log:conventions}).
Archived genomes allow rerunning the reported solvers without regenerating
them; an independent LLM search may produce different proposals.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
We followed the ICLR Code of Ethics. No new patient data or human-participant
experiments were collected. Retinal OCT images come from a previously released
dataset with randomized patient identifiers; we measure denoising computation,
not diagnostic accuracy. A solver's convergence certificate does not establish
clinical safety; consequential uses require independent application-specific
validation.

\section*{AI Use Statement}
Generative AI assisted with refining mathematical claims, proof writing,
feedback on methods and experiments, implementation and code editing,
literature analysis, identifying gaps, brainstorming improvements, manuscript
editing, keywords, and interpretation of the executable-loop experiments. It
was not used to generate synthetic data or provide critical proof ingredients.
Translation, data cleaning or reformatting,
and qualitative or thematic data analysis were not applicable.
We independently checked mathematical arguments and cited sources, reviewed
and tested assisted code, and take responsibility for all content and artifacts.
