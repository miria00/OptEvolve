\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Evolutionary and feedback-guided search.}
FunSearch and AlphaEvolve use language models to propose programs within
evaluation-driven search \citep{RomeraParedes2023Mathematical,Novikov2025AlphaEvolve}.
ReEvo combines evolutionary heuristic search with reflection
\citep{Ye2024ReEvo}, while GEPA uses execution feedback in prompt evolution
\citep{Agrawal2025GEPA}. These works establish the proposal, population and
feedback mechanisms. OptEvolve uses structured recipes whose mathematical
eligibility is checked before solver execution. Its current search loop is
implemented in the v2 package; OpenEvolve and SkyDiscover are related search
infrastructures, not the evaluator or engine used in the reported v2 hardware
diagnostics \citep{Sharma2025OpenEvolve,Liu2026SkyDiscover}.

\paragraph{Automated optimization research.}
AutoOPT combines BnB-PEP numerical design, LLM-assisted symbolic discovery,
Lean verification and human interpretation \citep{Kim2026AutoOPT}.
\citet{Yoon2026Lyapunov} connect computer-assisted convergence analysis with
Lyapunov proofs and identify an exact optimal proximal method for strongly
monotone inclusions. These are close precedents for algorithm and proof
discovery. OptEvolve currently searches within existing splitting constructions
and scores measured execution on a target distribution and device. Its Python
construction checker and numerical admission tests do not provide the
machine-checked proof assurance of Lean. We make no claim to introduce
automated optimization discovery or verification feedback itself.

\paragraph{Splittings and practical solver tuning.}
The mathematical substrate is the established theory of monotone-operator
splittings \citep{RyuYin2022LargeScale,ChambollePock2011First,Condat2013Primal,
Vu2013Splitting,DavisYin2017Three}. Relaxation, preconditioning and restarts
are established solver components, including in PDLP and OSQP
\citep{Applegate2021Practical,Stellato2020OSQP}. Termination-check frequency
is already configurable in PDLP \citep{ORToolsPDLP2026}; checking a certificate
less frequently is therefore a baseline implementation choice. Comparisons
must give competing recipes access to the same eligible execution policies.
The static PDHG configurations in our LP diagnostic are not the full adaptive
PDLP algorithm or a substitute for a production-solver comparison.

\paragraph{Hardware-aware kernel optimization.}
Hawkeye supplies coding agents with executable demonstrations of hardware
optimization strategies \citep{Hawkeye2026}. It motivates measured hardware
context and reference tests for implementation choices. Our current native
generator is restricted to FP64 CSR update templates for standard LP PDHG;
TV execution uses XLA-generated kernels. A numerical agreement test provides
evidence about an implementation, not a proof of floating-point refinement.
End-to-end solve time also includes certificate computation and detection
delay, so isolated kernel speed and launch count do not determine recipe
quality.
