\section{Conclusion}

OptEvolve searches executable optimization recipes with a mathematical
construction gate, hardware execution policies and numerical checks on the
original problem. The current diagnostics show that precision and checking
cadence materially affect measured execution, that fewer generated kernel
launches can still be slower, and that joint and sequential selection can
coincide. They do not yet demonstrate new algorithm discovery or an advantage
from LLM guidance. The central experimental question is whether joint selection
improves time to a checked solution beyond competent algorithm and
implementation tuning, under matched budgets and on independent instances.
